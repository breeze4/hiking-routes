Mike Sutak and Wendy Chase in Egypt 3.
Time:
Part 16.5 to 9.5 hours. This can be done as a long dayhike or a pleasant two-day hike.
Part 210.0 to 14.5 hours. Two days. Add another day if you plan to explore side canyons.
Water:
Water availability is not a problem on this hike. There is always water in Harris Wash and along the Escalante River.
