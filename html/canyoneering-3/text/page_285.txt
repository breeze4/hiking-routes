The Lamp Stand road starts 21.8 miles down the Burr Trail at a Lamp Stand sign (located at elevation 6294T on the Lamp Stand map). This graded road is suitable for high-clearance vehicles except after recent rains. Then even 4WDs will have problems. There is camping along the road. (See Map Thirty-eight.)
