50.8
Start of Clear Creek and the Cathedral in the Desert Hike #29. End of the two-wheel-drive road. 4WDs only past this point. From here it takes two hours to walk to the Hole-in-the-Rock. Many ride mountain bikes. Garfield County has proposed repairing the last section of road to the Hole-in-the-Rock, making it accessible to high-clearance vehicles. Check locally for current conditions. You are a quarter mile northeast of elevation 4315 and are on Navajo Sandstone.
