As you near the west side of the tower you will see a short roosterhead-shaped pinnacle behind the tower. Make your way to the pinnacle. Follow a bumpy ridge that divides two small canyons south-southwest to the top of the cliffs. There is one tricky downclimb (Class 3 +, 60') along the ridge. (1.0-1.5 hours.)
Back to the trailhead
Change course to due south. Follow the top of a gentle, sandy, pinyon-and-juniper-covered ridge for twenty-five minutes to a large area of white undulating Navajo slickrock. You can now see the escarpment you started on. (Tenmile Flat map.) The second drop described at the start of the hike (the red-and-white-banded slab) will also be visible.
Cross broken ground toward the second drop. Skirt washes and small canyons as necessary. You will lose sight of the second drop; but, as you near the escarpment, the white Navajo tower and pinnacle will appear. Follow upper Phipps Wash back to the trailhead. (2.0-3.0 hours.)
