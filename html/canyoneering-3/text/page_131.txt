Signed Tenmile Wash. The upper part of Tenmile Wash is called Alvey Wash. Tenmile Spring, a short walk downcanyon, is considered the start of Harris Wash.
The Hole-in-the-Rock expedition used Tenmile Springwhich is ten miles from Escalantefor their first campsite. Expedition member George Decker wrote in his diary: "Moved to 10 Miles Gulch, feed scarce, water up." A wagon road developed by ranchers ended at Tenmile Spring. A single-track trail continued on. The going started to get rough.
