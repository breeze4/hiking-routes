Bement Arch in Willow Gulch.
Digression: For those who left a car at the Willow Gulch trailhead, watch for a trail that climbs a sandy hillside to the left and goes by a formation that looks like a graduation cap, or mortarboard.
Continue up the ever-shallower canyon to the Hole-in-the-Rock road. Mosey a mile along the road back to the trailhead. (3.0-3.5 hours.)
