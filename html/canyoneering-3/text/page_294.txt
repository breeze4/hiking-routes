Jim Finch in the Poison Ivy Fork of Choprock Canyon.
From the confluence of the main and West forks of Choprock Canyon, hike down the main canyon for eighteen minutes. Look for a long row of Moqui steps on a southwest-facing wall on the right (LDC). You must look upcanyon to see them. Climb the steps (Class 5.4, 50') and then work your way to the top of the Wingate. Locate a route through the Kayenta. Memorize the route. Return to the mouth of the West Fork.
