Access to Hikes #7 through 9. 

Access is from Highway 12.

The Old Sheffield road starts at milepost 70.5 on Highway 12 (located at elevation 6001 on the Tenmile Flat map) . The road is suitable for high-clearance vehicles. There are campsites along the road. (See Map Five.)
